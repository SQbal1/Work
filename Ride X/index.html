Common questions

Ask on WhatsApp
How quickly will I get a response?

Requests are usually answered the same day during working hours. WhatsApp is the fastest channel for both new requests and follow-up.

Where does Ride X operate?

Jeddah is the primary service area, including King Abdulaziz International Airport. Trips beyond Jeddah can be requested and are confirmed based on availability.

Can I book an airport pickup?

Yes. Share your flight time, terminal, passenger count, and luggage in the quote form or on WhatsApp, and the pickup is planned around your arrival.

Do you handle family bookings?

Yes — family transportation is a core service. Mention who is travelling and any special needs, and the trip is arranged accordingly.

How is the price decided?

The quote is based on the route, timing, and vehicle type. You receive a clear price before anything is confirmed — the price you approve is the price you pay.

Can I book in advance?

Yes, and it is recommended. Advance booking gives the most flexibility for timing and vehicle choice, especially for airport transfers and group trips.
