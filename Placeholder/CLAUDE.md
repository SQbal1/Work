## Core Product Thesis

Placeholder is not meant to be a giant ERP from day one.

It is a simple, modern SaaS platform that helps SMEs and micro-businesses:

- create invoices quickly
- save customer information
- manage services/products
- track payment status
- reduce repetitive manual entry
- understand invoice completeness
- operate without deep accounting knowledge
- eventually support VAT/ZATCA-aware workflows

The MVP should compete through:

- clarity
- speed
- ease of use
- guided workflows
- niche-specific usefulness
- customer-led iteration

Do **not** compete through feature count.

## Current MVP Focus

The product should initially focus on a practical invoicing workflow for SMEs, especially consulting/professional service businesses, while keeping the architecture flexible for future business types.

Possible early niche:

- consulting businesses
- professional services
- small agencies
- freelancers
- later: logistics SMEs, retail, restaurants, construction

Initial workflow should be generic enough to support future expansion, but polished enough for a consulting/professional services demo.

---

## Product Philosophy

Build the smallest useful product that can be shown to real businesses.

The MVP is not trying to replace Zoho, Odoo, Wafeq, or Qoyod immediately.

The MVP should answer:

> Can a small business create invoices, manage customers, track payments, and understand what to do next without confusion?

The goal is to get customer feedback, not build a perfect system.

---

## What Not To Do

Do **not**:

- build a giant ERP
- support every industry deeply from day one
- overbuild inventory, payroll, HR, CRM, or accounting modules
- claim official ZATCA compliance
- build real ZATCA integration yet
- build real payment gateway integration yet
- build real WhatsApp/email sending yet
- overuse AI features
- add complex state management unless needed
- rewrite the whole app when improving one section
- use Areeco branding unless explicitly requested
- remove existing working pages or logic without reason

## Design Direction

Use a professional, trustworthy fintech/SaaS design.

Recommended aesthetic from DESIGN.md libraries:

- Technical UI
- Neutral Surfaces
- Utilitarian
- Enterprise dashboard
- Stripe / Linear / Mercury-like polish
- modern ERP without visual clutter

Avoid:

- generic purple SaaS template
- childish gradients
- excessive whitespace
- portfolio-style typography
- experimental type
- flashy AI-startup visuals

The UI should make users think:

> I can trust this with my business operations.
